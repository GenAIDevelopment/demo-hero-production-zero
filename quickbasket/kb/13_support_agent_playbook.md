# Support Agent Playbook v3.1 · Updated 3 October 2026 · Internal

*Fictional company for training use. All content synthetic.*

Owner: Customer Care Operations, QuickBasket. Questions about this playbook: care-ops@quickbasket.example.
This document is for QuickBasket's human support agents and Customer Care Leads. It is not shown to
customers. Do not paste parts of it into a chat, apart from the canned replies in section 8.

## 1. How to use this playbook

### 1.1 What this document is

The playbook turns the Customer Policy into steps you can follow while a customer is waiting in chat. It
tells you what to check, in what order, who can approve what, and which reply to send. It does not create
any new rule for customers. Everything a customer is entitled to comes from the Customer Policy and, for a
few local details, from the City Addenda.

Use it like this:

- New agents: read sections 1 to 7 once before your first shift. Keep section 3 open during chats.
- Every agent: when a chat arrives, follow section 2 first, every time, even when the customer is in a
  hurry.
- When you are not sure: look at the decision table. If you still do not know, say so to the customer (macro M-30) and ask your shift Lead.

### 1.2 The Customer Policy v4.2 always wins

The current policy is **Customer Policy v4.2, effective 1 October 2026**. If anything in this playbook, in a
macro, in an old chat transcript or in a colleague's memory disagrees with the Customer Policy v4.2, the
policy is right and the other thing is wrong. Follow the policy and report the difference to
care-ops@quickbasket.example so that it gets fixed.

The same applies to the automated assistant. It follows the same policy and the same approval limit as you
do. It is not allowed to promise anything the policy does not allow, and neither are you.

Other documents you will use next to this one:

- **City Addenda**: stores, delivery fees, store hours and local rules for the six cities.
- **Plus Membership Terms**, **Gift Cards and QuickBasket Cash**, **Promo Terms**: details the policy
  does not spell out.
- **Electronics Warranty Directory**: where to send customers once the 3-day electronics window is over.
- **Superseded policies v4.1, v4.0 and v3.2**: only for disputes about old orders. See section 6.

### 1.3 What changed on 1 October 2026 (v4.1 to v4.2)

### 1.4 Channels and hours

Support chat is open from 7:00 am to midnight, every day. Customers reach us from the order page in the app
or from Support chat. Chats from QuickBasket Plus members are answered first. The automated assistant
answers first on most chats and hands over to a human agent when the customer is unhappy with a decision,
when the language turns abusive or threatening, or when it does not know the answer.

We do not take support requests by phone. If a customer asks for a number to call, tell them that support
is through in-app chat and that you will stay with them until the issue is closed.

## 2. First steps on every chat

Do these four steps in this order before you discuss any order detail or take any action. This is a policy
requirement, not a courtesy: support, human or automated, must confirm the order belongs to the customer
before discussing or acting on it.

**Step 1. Get the order ID.**
The format is `QB-` followed by five digits, for example QB-12345. If the customer gives a name, an
address, an item or a date instead, ask for the ID. Use macro M-01. If the ID
is in the wrong format or opens nothing, use macro M-02. Do not
search for orders by name or address and read the results out to the customer.

**Step 2. Check the account match.**
Open the order in the helpdesk console. The console shows "Account match: Yes" when the order was placed
from the account the customer is chatting from, and "Account match: No" when it was not.

- Account match Yes: carry on to step 3.
- Account match No: stop. Do not confirm or deny anything about the order: not the items, not the address,
  not the status, not the amount. Send macro M-03. Requests can only be
  raised by the account that placed the order. A family member, flatmate, office colleague or security
  guard who has the order ID is still not the account holder.

**Step 3. Check the order status and the delivery time.**
Note whether the order is placed, assigned, picked up, out for delivery or delivered. If it is delivered,
note the delivery time shown in the app. Every return and report window is counted from that time, not
from when the customer opened the bag and not from when the order was placed.

**Step 4. Check the refund history.**
Look at two things: whether this order already has a refund, and how many refunds the account holds in the
last 30 days. Both change what you are allowed to do. See section 4.

Only after these four steps do you ask what went wrong. Then find the item's category, open the matching
row in section 3.1 and follow it.

## 3. Decision tables

These tables repeat the Customer Policy v4.2. They are here so that you do not need to open the PDF during
a chat. If you ever see a difference, the PDF is right.

### 3.1 Refund eligibility by category

Windows are counted from the delivery time shown in the app. The general rule is 7 days for unused items
in their original packaging, but the category rules below always take priority over the general rule.

| Category | Return or report window | What we offer | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh fruits & vegetables | Report within 24 hours | Refund or replacement only | Not returnable. Refund only if damaged, spoiled or missing. Photo required. |
| Dairy, bakery & eggs | Report within 24 hours | Refund or replacement only | Not returnable. Refund only if spoiled before the best-before date. |
| Frozen & meat | Report within 12 hours | Refund only | Not returnable. Must be reported with photo of packaging and temperature label. |
| Packaged food & beverages | 7 days | Return or refund | Unopened, before expiry date. |
| Personal care & beauty | 7 days | Return or refund | Seal unbroken. Opened items only if damaged on arrival. |
| Electronics & accessories | 3 days | Replacement first; refund if no replacement stock | Unused, all accessories and box included. Manufacturer warranty applies after 3 days. |
| Baby care | 7 days | Return or refund | Seal unbroken. Formula and food not returnable. |
| Gift cards & QuickBasket Plus | Not returnable | See Customer Policy Section 6 | Gift cards cannot be refunded or exchanged. |

How to read the table:

- "Report within" means the customer must tell us inside that time. The item is not collected.
- "Refund or replacement only" means the customer chooses one of the two. "Refund only" means we do not
  send a replacement.
- For fresh fruits and vegetables a photo is always required. For frozen and meat you need two photos: the
  packaging and the temperature label.
- For electronics, offer the replacement first. A refund is possible only when there is no replacement
  stock. After 3 days the manufacturer's warranty applies and we do not take the item back.
- Baby formula and baby food are never returnable, sealed or not.
- Gift cards are never refunded or exchanged. QuickBasket Plus follows its own cancellation rule (3.3).
- A refund is always the amount the customer actually paid for the item after any discount, not the
  listed price. Free gifts that came with an offer are not returnable and are not refunded.

Local exceptions from the City Addenda that touch this table:

- **Chennai, declared cyclone or heavy-rain advisory only:** for orders delivered during the advisory, the
  report window for fresh, dairy and frozen or meat items is 48 hours. This applies only in Chennai and
  only to orders delivered while the advisory was declared. It is not the general fresh produce window.
- **Bengaluru, QB Tech Hub stores (BLR-01, BLR-03, BLR-04):** an approved electronics replacement inside
  the 3-day window is delivered the same day if it is approved before 6 pm. The window itself is still
  3 days.

### 3.2 Late-delivery compensation

Every order shows a promised delivery time at checkout. Compare it with the delivery time shown in the
app.

| How late | All customers | QuickBasket Plus members |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 15 minutes | No compensation | No compensation |
| 16 to 30 minutes | Delivery fee refunded | Delivery fee refunded |
| More than 30 minutes | Delivery fee refunded | Delivery fee refunded plus ₹50 QuickBasket Cash |

Rules that go with the table:

- Compensation is credited automatically as QuickBasket Cash within 2 hours of delivery. Agents do not add
  it by hand. If it is missing after 2 hours, report it to the shift Lead.
- Compensation is always QuickBasket Cash. It is never a bank refund, whatever the payment method was.
- The ₹50 QuickBasket Cash is for QuickBasket Plus members only. A customer without Plus whose order is
  more than 30 minutes late gets the delivery fee back and nothing more.
- If the delivery was free on that order, the "delivery fee refunded" part is ₹0.
- Compensation Cash expires 180 days after it is credited.
- **Excluded:** delays caused by the customer (wrong address, customer not reachable, gated community
  entry) and declared weather or traffic advisories. Delivery partners wait up to 7 minutes at a gate.
  Time spent waiting for gate entry is a customer-side delay.
- Do not offer anything outside this table to calm a customer down. No extra credit, no coupon, no free
  delivery on the next order.

### 3.3 Cancellation

| Order status | Can it be cancelled? | Fee | What to tell the customer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Placed, no delivery partner assigned | Yes | Free | Cancel on request. No fee. |
| Delivery partner assigned, not yet picked up | Yes | ₹25 | Say the ₹25 fee out loud and get a clear yes before cancelling. |
| Out for delivery (picked up) | No | Not applicable | The customer can refuse the order at the door, then request a refund for unopened items once the order is marked delivered. |
| Delivered | No | Not applicable | Use the category rules in 3.1 for returns and refunds. |

QuickBasket Plus membership has its own rule. Plus costs ₹149 per month or ₹1,299 per year and gives free
delivery on orders above ₹199.

| Plus situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Cancelled within 48 hours of purchase, no free delivery used | Full refund |
| Cancelled within 48 hours of purchase, a free delivery already used | Can cancel. Current period not refunded. |
| Cancelled more than 48 hours after purchase | Can cancel any time. Current period not refunded. |

### 3.4 Refund timelines

Once a refund is approved, the money goes back to the original payment method. The customer can choose
QuickBasket Cash instead, which is instant.

| Original payment method | Refund lands in | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| UPI | 2–4 business days | Back to the same UPI-linked bank account. |
| Credit or debit card | 5–7 business days | Depends on the issuing bank. |
| QuickBasket Cash | Instant | Shows in the wallet immediately. |
| Cash on delivery | Instant, as QuickBasket Cash | Bank refund on request: 5–7 business days. |

Notes:

- Business days do not include Sundays and bank holidays. Do not promise a calendar date. Give the range.
- Refund Cash, which is QuickBasket Cash given as a refund, never expires.
- Money debited for a payment that failed, where no order was created, is not a refund from us. The
  customer's bank reverses it automatically in 3 to 5 business days. Use macro
  M-29.
- If a refund has not arrived after the upper end of the range, raise a ticket to the Payments Desk (see
  the escalation matrix). Do not issue the refund a second time.

## 4. Approval workflow

### 4.1 Who can approve what

| Refund amount (per order) | Who can approve | Extra checks |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹500 | Support agent or automated assistant | Order belongs to the customer, is delivered, and is eligible under the category rules. |
| Above ₹500 | Customer Care Lead (human) | All of the above, plus photo evidence review. |
| Any amount on an order already refunded | Not allowed | One refund per order. Escalate disputes. |

Two more rules apply to every refund, whatever the amount:

- **No refund before delivery.** An order that is not yet marked delivered cannot be refunded. Use macro
  M-17.
- **At most three refunds per customer in any 30-day period.** If the account already holds three refunds
  in the last 30 days, the next request goes to the Customer Care Lead, even if it is for ₹20 and clearly
  eligible. Use macro M-18.

The limit is per order, not per item. Three items of ₹200 each on one order make a ₹600 refund, and that
goes to the Customer Care Lead. Do not split one request into smaller refunds to stay under ₹500. The
console blocks it and it is logged against your agent ID.

### 4.2 What you can approve yourself (up to ₹500)

Before you press Approve, all five of these must be true:

1. Account match is Yes.
2. The order is marked delivered.
3. The item is eligible under its category row in 3.1, and the report is inside the window.
4. The required photo or photos are attached to the chat.
5. The order has no earlier refund, and the account holds fewer than three refunds in the last 30 days.

If any of the five is false, you do not approve. Either decline with the right macro or raise the request
to the Customer Care Lead, as the tables say.

### 4.3 How to raise an approval request to the Customer Care Lead

Use this for refunds above ₹500, for a fourth refund request within 30 days, for disputes on an order that
was already refunded, and for old-version disputes (section 6).

1. Finish the checks in section 2. A Lead will send back any request where the account match is not Yes.
2. For refunds above ₹500, collect photo evidence in the chat. Ask for a photo of each affected item, and
   for frozen or meat items also the temperature label. Check that the photos open and show the problem.
3. In the helpdesk console, open the order and choose **Raise approval**. This creates a request in the
   **CCL-Approvals** queue.
4. Fill in every field:
   - Order ID and refund amount requested.
   - Reason code: `ABOVE_LIMIT`, `REFUND_COUNT`, `ALREADY_REFUNDED_DISPUTE` or `OLD_VERSION`.
   - Category of each item and the time between delivery and report.
   - Your recommendation in one line: approve, decline or unsure, and why.
   - Photos attached: yes or no. For `ABOVE_LIMIT`, the answer must be yes.
5. Tell the customer what is happening with macro M-15 or
   M-18. Do not tell the customer the request "will be approved".
   Do not give the customer a time by which the Lead will reply.
6. Keep the chat open and stay assigned to it. The Lead's internal target is a first response within
   15 minutes during chat hours (7:00 am to midnight). If there is no response after 20 minutes, post the
   request ID in the shift channel `#care-leads-on-shift`.
7. When the Lead decides, the console shows Approved, Declined or More information needed. Pass the
   decision to the customer in your own words, with the refund timeline from 3.4 if it was approved.
8. If your shift ends before the decision, write a handover note (section 10) and reassign the chat to
   the incoming agent. Never leave an approval request without an owner.

The 15-minute and 20-minute figures are internal targets for our own team. They are not promises to the
customer and must not be quoted in a chat.

### 4.4 Orders that were already refunded

One refund per order. If a customer asks for a second refund on the same order, the answer is no, whatever
the reason and whatever the amount. Send macro M-16.

If the customer disputes the first refund (wrong amount, wrong item refunded, refund not received), do not
try to fix it with a second refund. Raise an approval request with the reason code
`ALREADY_REFUNDED_DISPUTE`, or a Payments Desk ticket if the refund was approved but the money has not
arrived after the timeline in 3.4.

## 5. Escalation matrix

The policy says that a customer who is unhappy with a decision, or who uses abusive or threatening
language, is handed to a human agent. For human agents the next step up is the shift Customer Care Lead.
The "how fast" column is an internal target for our team, not a promise to the customer.

| Situation | Who takes it | How fast (internal target) | What you do first |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abusive language towards the agent | Shift Customer Care Lead | Hand over within 5 minutes of the second abusive message | Send M-32 once. If it continues, transfer the chat. Do not argue. |
| Threat of harm to an agent, a delivery partner or store staff | Shift Customer Care Lead, who informs the Trust & Safety Desk | Immediately. No warning macro. | Transfer the chat, tag it `threat`, and write down the exact words used in the handover note. |
| Repeated refund requests (three refunds already in the last 30 days) | Customer Care Lead | Raise within the same chat. Lead target: 15 minutes. | Raise an approval request (section 4.3). Send M-18. |
| Refund above ₹500 | Customer Care Lead | Raise within the same chat. Lead target: 15 minutes. | Collect photos, raise an approval request, send M-15. |
| Dispute about an order that was already refunded | Customer Care Lead | Same shift | Do not refund again. Send M-16 and raise the dispute. |
| Payment dispute (double charge, chargeback notice, refund not received after the stated timeline) | Payments Desk, through the `PAY-Disputes` queue | Ticket raised within 30 minutes. Desk replies within 1 business day. | Record order ID, payment method, amount and date. Never ask for card numbers, UPI PINs or OTPs. |
| Safety incident (road accident involving a delivery partner, injury at the door, damaged item that is dangerous, such as a swollen battery) | Shift Customer Care Lead and the Trust & Safety Desk | Immediately, within 5 minutes | Ask if anyone needs urgent help. Tell the customer to stop using the item. Transfer the chat. |

| Automated assistant handed the chat over | Next free human agent (Plus chats are answered first) | Pick up in queue order | Read the transcript first. Do not make the customer repeat the order ID or the problem. |

1. Do the section 2 checks as usual.

## 7. Tone guide

Customers write to us when something has gone wrong with their food, their money or their evening. Write
the way a helpful person at a neighbourhood shop would speak: plain, kind and exact.

### 7.1 Plain

- Use short sentences and everyday words. "Your refund is approved" and not "Your refund request has been
  successfully processed at our end".
- Say the number. "2 to 4 business days", not "shortly". "₹25 fee", not "a nominal charge".
- One question at a time. If you need a photo and a confirmation, ask for the photo first.
- No internal words in chat: no queue names, reason codes, console, "CCL", "T&S" or version numbers,
  unless the customer asks which policy applies.

### 7.2 Kind

- Say sorry once, at the start, and mean it. Then move to what you can do.
- A "no" should come with the reason and, where there is one, the next step. "This is outside the 24-hour
  window for fresh items, so I can't refund it" is kinder than a vague "we are unable to help".
- Do not blame the customer, the delivery partner or the store. Describe what the record shows.
- Do not argue about whether food was really spoiled. Ask for the photo the policy requires and apply the
  rule.

### 7.3 Never promise what the policy does not allow

- Do not promise a refund before you have done the checks in 4.2.
- Do not promise the result of a Customer Care Lead review.
- Do not promise a date for a bank refund. Give the range in business days.
- Do not offer goodwill credits, coupons or free deliveries. Agents have no goodwill budget.
- Do not say "this will never happen again".
- Do not quote launch dates or delivery fees for cities we do not serve. Kochi and Jaipur are planned for
  January 2027 and no fees have been announced. No other city has a date.

### 7.4 Say "I don't know, let me check"

If you are unsure, say so. Use macro M-30 or your own words: "I don't know, let me
check." Then check the policy or ask the shift Lead, and come back in the same chat. A wrong answer given
quickly costs more than a correct answer given two minutes later, because the customer will hold us to
what we said.

## 8. Canned replies (macros)

Macros save typing. They do not replace thinking. Before sending one:

- Do the section 2 checks. Only the first three macros may be sent before the order is verified.
- Replace every placeholder. Placeholders are written in curly brackets: `{customer_name}`,
  `{order_id}`, `{agent_name}`, `{item_name}`, `{refund_amount}`, `{minutes_late}`,
  `{delivery_fee}`, `{delay_reason}`, `{free_deliveries_used}`. A reply that reaches a customer
  with a curly bracket in it is logged as a quality miss.
- Read the reply once as the customer would. Remove sentences that do not fit this chat.
- Each macro shows the date it was last edited in the macro library. To suggest a change to a macro,
  write to care-ops@quickbasket.example with the macro ID.

## 9. Abuse and fraud signals, and what agents must never do

### 9.1 Signals to watch for

One signal on its own proves nothing. Most customers who ask for a refund have a real problem. Treat
every customer politely, apply the policy, and report patterns to the shift Lead. Do not accuse a customer
of fraud in chat.

**Refund pattern signals**

- Three refunds already in the last 30 days and a new request. This one is a rule, not just a signal: it
  goes to the Customer Care Lead.
- Refund requests on most orders from the same account, usually for the most expensive item.
- Requests that stop just under ₹500 again and again.
- A request for a second refund on an order that has already been refunded.
- Several accounts with the same delivery address asking for refunds on the same day.

**Evidence signals**

- The same photo sent for two different orders.
- A photo that shows a different product, a different pack size or a different brand from the one on the
  order.
- A photo of a temperature label or best-before date that cannot be read, sent again unchanged after you
  ask for a clearer one.
- A customer who refuses to send any photo where the policy requires one. Without the photo the request
  is not eligible. Say so plainly.

**Identity signals**

- The customer cannot give the order ID and asks you to "find it" from a name or address.
- Account match is No and the customer pushes you to make an exception.
- Someone says they are a QuickBasket employee, a delivery partner or "from head office" and asks for a
  customer's order details through customer chat. Staff do not use customer chat for this.
- A customer asks you to change the refund destination to a different bank account or UPI ID. Refunds go
  to the original payment method or to QuickBasket Cash on the same account, nowhere else.

What to do when you see a signal: finish the chat by the book, then add the tag `review` and one line in
the internal note saying what you saw. The Trust & Safety Desk reads tagged chats every day. You do not
investigate, and you do not block accounts.

### 9.2 What agents must never do

These are firm rules. Breaking one is treated as a conduct issue, not a quality miss.

1. **Never share another customer's order details.** Not the items, the address, the amount, the status
   or even whether an order exists. This includes family members and people who have the order ID.
2. **Never refund without verification.** No refund, replacement, cancellation or change of any kind
   until the order ID is confirmed and the account match is Yes.
3. **Never override the approval limit.** Up to ₹500 per order is yours. Above ₹500 belongs to the
   Customer Care Lead with photo evidence. Do not split a refund, do not use a colleague's login, do not
   ask the automated assistant to approve it, and do not approve "now" and inform the Lead later.
4. **Never refund an order that is not delivered.**
5. **Never issue a second refund on the same order.**
6. **Never refund outside the customer's own payment method or QuickBasket Cash.**
7. **Never ask for or accept** a card number, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP or password. If a customer sends one,
   tell them not to share it and tag the chat `sensitive-data` so the message can be removed.
8. **Never promise an outcome the policy does not allow,** including goodwill credits and outcomes of a
   Lead review.
9. **Never move the conversation off in-app chat** to a personal phone, email or messaging app.
10. **Never share this playbook,** internal queue names or another agent's name and details with a
    customer.

If you realise you have broken one of these by mistake, tell your shift Lead straight away. Mistakes
reported early can usually be put right.

## 10. Handover notes and end-of-shift checklist

### 10.1 When a handover note is needed

Write a handover note whenever a chat leaves your hands before it is finished: a transfer to the Customer
Care Lead, an approval request still waiting when your shift ends, a Payments Desk ticket, or a customer
who has to come back with a photo. The next person must be able to act without asking the customer to
repeat anything.

### 10.2 Handover note template

Copy this into the internal note on the chat and fill in every line. Write "none" where a line does not
apply. Do not leave a line empty.

```
HANDOVER NOTE
Order ID:                 QB-_____
Account match:            Yes / No
Order status:             placed / assigned / picked up / out for delivery / delivered
Delivery time in app:     ____ (date and time)
Order date and policy:    ____ (v4.2 unless the order was placed before 1 October 2026)
Customer's issue:         ____ (one or two plain sentences, in the customer's words where possible)
Item(s) and category:     ____
Time from delivery to report: ____ hours / days
Photos received:          Yes / No (what they show)
Refund amount requested:  ₹____
Refunds on this order:    none / already refunded on ____
Refunds on account, last 30 days: ____
What I have done:         ____
What I have told the customer: ____ (exact promise made, if any; write "no promise made" otherwise)
Approval request:         none / request ID ____ in CCL-Approvals, reason code ____
Other tickets:            none / PAY-Disputes ticket ____ / food-safety / threat
What needs to happen next: ____
Who owns it now:          ____
Tags added:               ____
Handed over by:           ____ (agent name), at ____ (time)
```

A good handover note is short and complete. A poor one says "customer angry, pls check". If you receive a
poor note, ask the sender to fix it while they are still on shift.

### 10.3 End-of-shift checklist

Run through this list in the last 15 minutes of your shift.

- [ ] No chat is still assigned to me without a handover note.
- [ ] Every approval request I raised in CCL-Approvals has a decision passed to the customer, or has been
      reassigned to an agent on the next shift with a note.
- [ ] Every customer who is waiting for something has been told what happens next, without a promise the
      policy does not allow.
- [ ] Every Payments Desk ticket I raised has the order ID, payment method, amount and date.
- [ ] Chats with threats, safety incidents or medical complaints about food have been transferred to the
      shift Lead and tagged. None are sitting in my queue.
- [ ] Chats where I saw a fraud signal are tagged `review` with one line saying what I saw.
- [ ] Any message in which a customer shared a card number, PIN or OTP is tagged `sensitive-data`.
- [ ] I have reported any macro or playbook line that disagrees with Customer Policy v4.2 to
      care-ops@quickbasket.example.
- [ ] I have not left photos, order details or customer data in personal notes, screenshots or downloads.
- [ ] I have set my status to Offline and logged out of the helpdesk console.

Leads also check at the end of each shift that the CCL-Approvals queue has no request older than
30 minutes without a first response, and that the incoming Lead knows about every open threat, safety or
food-safety chat.
