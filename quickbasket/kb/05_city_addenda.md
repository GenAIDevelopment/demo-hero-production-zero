### Common questions in Hyderabad

**Why does my promised time change in the evening?**  
Between 7 pm and 10 pm the app recalculates promised times every few minutes based on traffic on the Outer Ring Road and inner-city roads. The time shown when you place the order is the one used for late-delivery compensation.

**Can I order from Secunderabad if my office is in Gachibowli?**  
Orders are served by the store that covers the delivery address, so you can order to any serviceable address in the city.
