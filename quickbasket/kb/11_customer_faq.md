## 1. Ordering and account

*Last reviewed: 8 October 2026*

How ordering works, what you need for a request, and how your account is used.

In short:

- Every request needs the order ID (format QB-12345) and must come from the account that placed the order.
- Support confirms the order is yours before discussing it.
- An order can't be edited after it is placed; cancel and reorder instead.
- Prices include GST; the invoice is under Orders › Invoice.

**How do I place an order?**

Choose your delivery address, add items to the basket and go to checkout. The checkout screen shows the item total, any discount, the delivery fee, any surcharge and the promised delivery time before you pay. The order is placed once the payment goes through, or straight away for cash on delivery.

**What can I buy on QuickBasket?**

Groceries, household essentials and electronics. That includes fresh fruits and vegetables, dairy, bakery and eggs, frozen food and meat, packaged food and beverages, personal care and beauty, baby care, electronics and accessories, and QuickBasket gift cards.

**Is there a minimum order value?**

No. You can order a single item. Smaller orders pay a higher delivery fee, though: the fee depends on your city and on the order value after discounts, and the Cities and coverage section lists it for each city.

**where do i find my order id**

Open Orders in the app and tap the order. The order ID is at the top and looks like QB-12345. You need it for every return, refund or missing-item request.

**why can't i see an item that my friend can see in the app**

Each store carries a slightly different range, and the app shows only what the store covering your delivery address has in stock. Your friend's address is probably served by a different store.

**Can I change the delivery address after placing an order?**

No. The address can't be edited once the order is placed, because the order is already with the store that covers that address. Cancel the order, which is free until a delivery partner is assigned, and place it again with the right address.

**Can I add items to an order I have already placed?**

No. An order can't be changed after it is placed. Place a second order for the extra items; it is charged and delivered separately.

**An item was missing from my order. What should I do?**

Raise a missing-item request from the order page, or tell Support in chat, and include the order ID. The reporting window of the item's category applies, so report missing fresh, dairy or frozen items quickly. The Returns and refunds section lists the window for each category.

**Can my husband raise a refund request from his phone for an order I placed?**

Only if he is signed in to the account that placed the order. Requests can be raised only by the account that placed the order, and Support will not discuss an order with a different account.

**Why does Support ask me to confirm the order before helping?**

Support agents, human or automated, have to confirm that an order belongs to you before they discuss its details or take any action on it. It protects you from someone else asking about, changing or refunding your orders.

**How do I get an invoice for my order?**

Every delivered order has a GST invoice under Orders › Invoice. Prices in the app already include GST. For electronics, the same invoice works as proof of purchase for the manufacturer's warranty.

**Can I add my company's GSTIN to an invoice?**

Yes, if you add it before ordering, under Account › Business details. It can't be added to an invoice after the order has been placed.

**can i order for my parents who live in another city**

Yes, as long as their address is in one of the six cities we serve. The delivery fee, store hours and any local rules of their city apply to that order. Any request about the order still has to come from your account, because you placed it.

**Can I leave instructions for the delivery partner?**

Yes. Use the order notes at checkout for things like a landmark, a gate code or "leave at the lobby". Notes can't change what is in the order or where it is going.

**Can I tip the delivery partner?**

Yes, tips are optional and the full amount goes to the delivery partner. A tip is refunded in full if the order is cancelled before pickup. Tips don't count towards the order value for offers or free delivery.

**Can I have more than one QuickBasket account?**

No. It is one account per person. Offers, QuickBasket Plus and QuickBasket Cash are linked to the account and can't be moved to another one.

**do the prices in the app include tax**

Yes. All prices shown in the app include GST. The delivery fee and any surcharge are shown as separate lines on the bill before you pay.

**I entered the wrong address and the order is already on its way. What can I do?**

An order that is out for delivery can't be cancelled or sent to a different address. Call the delivery partner from the order page and explain; any time lost because of a wrong address counts as a customer-side delay. If the order can't be handed over, refuse it and request a refund for the unopened items once it is marked delivered.

**Can I order electronics and groceries in the same order?**

Yes. One order can mix categories. If something goes wrong, each item follows the rule of its own category, so the milk and the charger in the same bag have different windows and different remedies.

**Do I have to return the whole order to get a refund for one item?**

No. Requests are raised item by item. The approval limit is counted per order, though: refunds of up to ₹500 on one order can be approved by a support agent or the automated assistant, and anything above that goes to the Customer Care Lead.

**how do i change my phone number**

Go to Account › Profile, enter the new number and confirm it with the OTP sent to that number. Your orders, Plus membership and QuickBasket Cash stay with the account.

**Can I reorder something I bought last week?**

Yes. Open the old order under Orders and tap Reorder. The basket is filled with the same items at today's prices, and anything the store doesn't have in stock right now is left out.

**What are household essentials, and which return rule do they follow?**

Things like detergent, cleaning supplies, kitchen rolls and stationery. They have no category rule of their own, so the general rule applies: they can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging.

## 2. Delivery and tracking

*Last reviewed: 15 October 2026*

Following an order from the store to your door, and what happens at gates, offices and in bad weather.

In short:

- The promised delivery time shown at checkout is the one that counts.
- Delivery fees depend on the city and on the order value after discounts.
- Partners wait up to 7 minutes at a gate; waiting for entry is a customer-side delay.
- Out for delivery means it can't be cancelled, only refused at the door.

**How fast is delivery?**

It depends on the store, the distance and the time of day. Every order shows a promised delivery time at checkout, before you pay, and that is the time we are held to.

**How do I track my order?**

Open the order in the app. It moves through four stages: placed, delivery partner assigned, picked up (out for delivery) and delivered. Once a partner has picked it up you can follow them on the map.

**where is my order it still says out for delivery**

Out for delivery means the partner has picked the order up and is on the way; the order page shows their position on the map. An order at this stage can't be cancelled. If it arrives after the promised time, late-delivery compensation is worked out automatically, so you don't need to raise anything.

**what does "Placed" mean on the order page**

The store has the order and is packing it. No delivery partner has been assigned yet, so you can still cancel free of charge.

**My order status says "Delivery partner assigned". What does that mean?**

A partner is on the way to the store or waiting for the order. You can still cancel until pickup, but a ₹25 fee applies.

**My order status says "Picked up". What does that mean?**

The partner has collected the order and is on the way to you; this is the same as out for delivery. The order can't be cancelled now, though you can refuse it at the door.

**what does "Delivered" mean on the order page**

The order has been handed over, and the delivery time shown here starts every return or report window. It can't be cancelled; raise a return or refund request if something is wrong.

**How much is the delivery fee?**

It depends on your city and on the order value after discounts. Each city has three bands: a fee for small orders below ₹199, a lower fee for mid-size orders, and free delivery above a threshold. The Cities and coverage section gives the amounts for every city.

**Is the delivery fee based on the price before or after my discount?**

After. The order value used for delivery fees is the item total after discounts. Tips and surcharges are not part of it.

**The promised time changed while I was shopping. Which one counts?**

The time shown when you place the order. Promised times are recalculated every few minutes at busy hours, so the estimate can move while you browse, but the one on the checkout screen when you pay is the one used for late-delivery compensation.

**I live in a gated community. How does delivery work?**

The delivery partner comes to the gate and needs entry, usually through an OTP or an approval on the society's visitor app. Partners wait up to 7 minutes at the gate. Time spent waiting for gate entry counts as a delay on the customer's side, so it is not counted when we check whether an order was late.

**Will the delivery partner come up to my office desk?**

In most office campuses and tech parks the order is handed over at the reception, the security desk or the visitor desk of the building named in the address, not at individual desks. Put the building name and tower in the address so the partner goes to the right desk.

**What happens if I don't answer when the delivery partner calls?**

The partner tries to reach you through the app and by phone. Any time lost because you could not be reached counts as a delay on the customer's side and is excluded from late-delivery compensation.

**the app says delivered but i did not get my order**

Check with your security desk, reception or neighbours first, because orders are sometimes handed over there. If it is not there, chat with Support straight away with the order ID. Support confirms the order is yours and checks the handover with the delivery partner.

**I got the wrong item. What do I do?**

Report it from the order page or in Support chat with the order ID and a photo of what you received. The window of the item's category applies, counted from the delivery time shown in the app.

**Can I refuse an order at the door?**

Yes. An order that is out for delivery can't be cancelled, but you can refuse it when it arrives. Once the order is marked delivered, request a refund for the unopened items from the order page.

**How do I contact my delivery partner?**

Once a partner is assigned, the order page has a call button and a message button. Calls go through the app, so neither of you sees the other's phone number.

**Do you deliver when it is raining heavily?**

Usually yes, but during a declared weather or traffic advisory promised times are longer and some localities may be paused. The app shows a banner while an advisory is active and tells you before you pay if your locality is paused, so no order is accepted that can't be delivered.

**How do I know if a weather advisory is active in my city?**

A banner appears at the top of the app and on the order page for as long as the advisory is declared for your city. Late-delivery compensation does not apply to orders delivered during a declared advisory.

**Which store does my order come from?**

The store that covers your delivery address. You can't pick a store yourself. This is also why the range, the opening hours and the promised time can differ between two addresses in the same city.

**The partner left my order at the lobby. Is that allowed?**

In some apartment complexes the residents' association doesn't allow delivery partners beyond the lobby or the security desk, and the order is handed over there. If your complex allows doorstep delivery, mention it in the order notes.

**Is anything charged on top of the delivery fee?**

A surcharge, where one applies, is shown as its own line on the bill before you pay. The one customers ask about most is the ₹35 late-night surcharge in Delhi NCR, on orders placed between 11 pm and 6 am from the 24-hour stores. A surcharge is separate from the delivery fee, so free delivery, including Plus free delivery, does not remove it.

**My order was late and one item was spoiled. Do I raise one request or two?**

One. Late-delivery compensation is worked out automatically, so there is nothing to raise for the delay. For the spoiled item, raise a request from the order page with a photo, inside the window for that item's category.

## 3. Late deliveries

*Last reviewed: 12 March 2026*

What we do when an order arrives after the promised delivery time.

In short:

- Up to 15 minutes late: no compensation.
- 16 to 30 minutes late: delivery fee refunded.
- More than 30 minutes late: delivery fee refunded plus ₹50 QuickBasket Cash, for every customer.
- Credited automatically as QuickBasket Cash within 2 hours of delivery; never a bank refund.
- Customer-caused delays and declared weather or traffic advisories are excluded.

**What happens if my order is late?**

We compensate automatically, based on how late the order was compared with the promised delivery time shown at checkout:

| How late | What you get |
|---|---|
| Up to 15 minutes | No compensation |
| 16 to 30 minutes | Delivery fee refunded |
| More than 30 minutes | Delivery fee refunded plus ₹50 QuickBasket Cash |

This applies to every customer. Compensation is credited as QuickBasket Cash, never as a bank refund.

**my order came 40 minutes late what do i get**

For an order more than 30 minutes late, we refund the delivery fee and add ₹50 QuickBasket Cash. Every customer gets this, whether or not they have QuickBasket Plus. Both amounts are credited to your wallet automatically.

**Do I get ₹50 if my order is more than half an hour late?**

Yes. Every customer whose order arrives more than 30 minutes after the promised time gets the delivery fee refunded plus ₹50 QuickBasket Cash. You don't need to be a Plus member and you don't need to ask.

**My order was 22 minutes late. What compensation do I get?**

An order that is 16 to 30 minutes late gets the delivery fee refunded as QuickBasket Cash. The ₹50 is added only when the order is more than 30 minutes late.

**order was 10 min late, any compensation?**

No. There is no compensation for delays of up to 15 minutes.

**Do I have to ask for late-delivery compensation?**

No. It is worked out and credited automatically, within 2 hours of the delivery. If it hasn't appeared in your QuickBasket Cash after that, chat with Support and share the order ID.

**Can the late-delivery compensation be paid to my bank account or UPI?**

No. Late-delivery compensation is always credited as QuickBasket Cash, never as a bank refund. This is true for the delivery fee refund and for the ₹50.

**How do you decide how late an order was?**

We compare the delivery time recorded in the app with the promised delivery time that was shown at checkout when you placed the order. Time lost to a delay on the customer's side is taken out first.

**My Hyderabad order of ₹320 was promised for 7:10 pm and arrived at 7:51 pm. What compensation do I get?**

That is 41 minutes late. For more than 30 minutes late we refund the delivery fee, ₹19 on a ₹320 order in Hyderabad, and add ₹50 QuickBasket Cash, so ₹69 in total. It is credited to your wallet automatically, like for any other customer. If part of the delay was waiting at your gate, that time is taken out first.

**Promised 8:30 am, delivered 8:54 am, order value ₹180, Mumbai. Am I owed anything?**

That is 24 minutes late. For 16 to 30 minutes late we refund the delivery fee, which was ₹39 on a ₹180 order in Mumbai. It is credited as QuickBasket Cash within 2 hours of delivery. If part of the delay was waiting at your gate, that time is taken out first.

**Promised 8:05 pm, delivered 8:40 pm, order value ₹260, Chennai. Am I owed anything?**

That is 35 minutes late. For more than 30 minutes late we refund the delivery fee, ₹15 on a ₹260 order in Chennai, and add ₹50 QuickBasket Cash, so ₹65 in total. It is credited to your wallet automatically, like for any other customer.

**My Pune order of ₹150 was promised for 7:45 am and arrived at 7:57 am. What compensation do I get?**

That is 12 minutes late. Delays of up to 15 minutes are not compensated, so nothing is credited for this order. If part of the delay was waiting at your gate, that time is taken out first.

**My Bengaluru order of ₹640 was promised for 9:20 pm and arrived at 10:12 pm. What compensation do I get?**

That is 52 minutes late. A ₹640 order in Bengaluru has free delivery, so there is no fee to refund, but because the order was more than 30 minutes late you receive ₹50 QuickBasket Cash, credited automatically.

**Promised 1:15 pm, delivered 1:33 pm, order value ₹410, Delhi NCR. Am I owed anything?**

That is 18 minutes late. For 16 to 30 minutes late we refund the delivery fee, which was ₹19 on a ₹410 order in Delhi NCR. It is credited as QuickBasket Cash within 2 hours of delivery.

**Promised 6:40 pm, delivered 7:13 pm, order value ₹450, Mumbai. Am I owed anything?**

That is 33 minutes late. For more than 30 minutes late we refund the delivery fee, ₹25 on a ₹450 order in Mumbai, and add ₹50 QuickBasket Cash, so ₹75 in total. It is credited to your wallet automatically, like for any other customer. If part of the delay was waiting at your gate, that time is taken out first.

**I ordered ₹120 of groceries in Chennai. The app said 10:00 am but it came at 10:28 am. Is there compensation?**

That is 28 minutes late. For 16 to 30 minutes late we refund the delivery fee, which was ₹30 on a ₹120 order in Chennai. It is credited as QuickBasket Cash within 2 hours of delivery.

**Which delays don't count?**

Delays caused by the customer and declared advisories. Customer-caused delays are a wrong address, the customer not being reachable, and waiting for entry at a gated community. Orders delivered during a declared weather or traffic advisory are also excluded from compensation.

**The delivery partner was stuck at my society gate for 10 minutes. Is the order counted as late?**

Waiting for gate entry is a delay on the customer's side, so that time is not counted against the promised delivery time. Partners wait up to 7 minutes at a gate. To avoid this, approve the entry request or share the gate OTP as soon as it arrives.

**I had free delivery on the order and it came late. Do I still get something?**

If there was no delivery fee on the order, there is no fee to refund. If the order was more than 30 minutes late, you still receive the ₹50 QuickBasket Cash.

**Is the ₹50 for each item or for the whole order?**

For the whole order. A late order gets one delivery fee refund and, if it was more than 30 minutes late, one credit of ₹50 QuickBasket Cash, whatever the number of items.

**Does late-delivery compensation expire?**

Yes. It is credited as Compensation Cash in your QuickBasket Cash wallet and expires 180 days after it is credited. It can pay for up to 100% of an order.

**my order was late, can i get a full refund for it**

No. A late delivery is compensated as described in this section; it does not make the items refundable. If something in the order was damaged, spoiled or missing, raise a separate request for that item under the rules for its category.

**It was raining and my order came 45 minutes late. Why was nothing credited?**

If a weather advisory was declared for your city at that time, the order is excluded from late-delivery compensation. The app shows a banner while an advisory is active. If no advisory was declared and nothing was credited within 2 hours of delivery, chat with Support with the order ID.

## 4. Returns and refunds by category

*Last reviewed: 6 October 2026*

The general rule first, then fresh produce, dairy, frozen food, packaged food, personal care and baby care in turn. Electronics have their own section below.

In short:

- General rule: 7 days, unused, original packaging. Category rules take priority.
- Fresh produce and dairy, bakery & eggs: report within 24 hours. Frozen & meat: within 12 hours.
- Packaged food, personal care and baby care: 7 days, unopened or seal unbroken.
- Refunds up to ₹500 per order: support agent or automated assistant. Above ₹500: Customer Care Lead.
- One refund per order, at most three refunds in any 30-day period, and none before delivery.

**What is your return policy in short?**

Most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging. Some categories have their own rules because of food safety, hygiene or manufacturer terms, and those rules always take priority over the general 7-day rule. All windows are counted from the delivery time shown in the app.

**Is the return window the same for every product?**

No. The category rules are:

| Category | Window | What we offer |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh fruits & vegetables | Report within 24 hours | Refund or replacement only; not returnable |
| Dairy, bakery & eggs | Report within 24 hours | Refund or replacement only; not returnable |
| Frozen & meat | Report within 12 hours | Refund only; not returnable |
| Packaged food & beverages | 7 days | Return or refund |
| Personal care & beauty | 7 days | Return or refund |
| Electronics & accessories | 3 days | Replacement first; refund if no replacement stock |
| Baby care | 7 days | Return or refund; formula and food not returnable |
| Gift cards & QuickBasket Plus | Not returnable | See the Plus and gift card sections |

The questions below go through the conditions for each grocery category.

**How do I raise a return or refund request?**

Open the order in the app and choose the item, or chat with QuickBasket Support. Every request needs the order ID (format QB-12345), and damaged or spoiled items need a photo. The request has to come from the account that placed the order.

**From when is the return window counted?**

From the delivery time shown in the app for that order, not from the time you placed the order or opened the bag. A 24-hour window on an order delivered at 6:10 pm ends at 6:10 pm the next day.

**I placed my order in September. Do today's rules apply to it?**

An order is governed by the Customer Policy version that was in force on the day the order was placed. The current version, v4.2, applies to orders placed from 1 October 2026. Older versions are kept, clearly marked as superseded, only so that questions about older orders can be settled under the rules of their time.

**What counts as fresh fruits and vegetables?**

Items such as mangoes, tomatoes, bananas, spinach and grapes. Every item page shows its category, and the same category is printed next to the item on the order page. The category decides which window applies; for fresh fruits and vegetables it is 24 hours from the delivery time shown in the app.

**How long do I have to report a problem with grapes?**

Report it within 24 hours of the delivery time shown in the app. This covers fresh fruits and vegetables that arrive damaged, spoiled or missing. A request raised after 24 hours is not eligible, so it is worth checking fresh items when they arrive. Keep the order ID (format QB-12345) handy; every request needs it.

**order with mangoes delivered 6:40 pm on 9 November, what is the last time to report an issue**

Until 6:40 pm on 10 November. The window for fresh fruits and vegetables is 24 hours, counted from the delivery time shown in the app, to the minute. It doesn't matter when the order was placed or when you opened the bag.

**Do you refund or replace bad mangoes?**

A refund or a replacement; you can ask for either when you raise the request. Fresh fruits and vegetables are not returnable, so nobody comes to collect the item. This applies only when the item was damaged, spoiled or missing and you report it within 24 hours of delivery.

**is a photo compulsory for bananas complaints**

Yes. A photo is required for every fresh fruit and vegetable request. Add it on the order page, or send it in Support chat together with the order ID (format QB-12345).

**My order arrived without the tomatoes. How do I get my money back?**

Raise a missing-item request from the order page, or in Support chat, with the order ID. Do it within 24 hours of the delivery time shown in the app, the same window as for other fresh fruits and vegetables requests. Once Support has confirmed the order is yours and the item was not delivered, you get a refund for it, or a replacement if you prefer.

**I forgot to report my bananas in time. Can I still get a refund?**

No. Fresh fruits and vegetables have to be reported within 24 hours of the delivery time shown in the app, and a later request is not eligible for a refund or a replacement. The one exception is an order delivered in Chennai during a declared cyclone or heavy-rain advisory, where the window is 48 hours.

**i ordered too much spinach, can i send it back**

No. Fresh fruits and vegetables are not returnable. We refund or replace them only when they arrive damaged, spoiled or missing, and only when that is reported within 24 hours with a photo.

**Which items fall under dairy, bakery and eggs?**

Items such as milk, paneer, curd, bread and eggs. Every item page shows its category, and the same category is printed next to the item on the order page. The category decides which window applies; for dairy, bakery and eggs it is 24 hours from the delivery time shown in the app.

Within 24 hours of the delivery time shown in the app. The same 24-hour window applies to everything in Dairy, bakery & eggs. After that the request is not eligible. You can raise it from the order page at any hour, or in Support chat between 7:00 am and midnight.

**My eggs was delivered at 9:30 pm on 11 November. Until when can I report a problem?**

Until 9:30 pm on 12 November. The window for dairy, bakery and eggs is 24 hours, counted from the delivery time shown in the app, to the minute. The order page shows the delivery time, so you can check it there.

**Do you refund or replace bad bread?**

A refund or a replacement. Dairy, bakery and eggs are not returnable, and we refund or replace them only if they were spoiled before the best-before date printed on the pack. The problem has to be reported within 24 hours of delivery.

## 12. Safety, privacy and talking to a human

*Last reviewed: 28 October 2026*

Reaching Support, what the automated assistant can and can't do, and keeping your account safe.

In short:

- Support chat: 7:00 am to midnight, every day, in the app.
- Unhappy with a decision? The conversation is handed to a human agent.
- The automated assistant never promises what the Customer Policy doesn't allow.
- QuickBasket never asks for your UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or bank OTP.

**How do I contact QuickBasket Support?**

Through the in-app chat, under Help. Support chat is open from 7:00 am to midnight, every day. Have your order ID ready if the question is about an order.

**Is there a phone number I can call?**

No. Support is handled in the in-app chat so that every conversation is tied to your account and your order. For questions about the Customer Policy itself you can write to policy@quickbasket.example.

**How do I talk to a human instead of the automated assistant?**

Ask for a human agent in the chat. The conversation is also handed to a human agent whenever a customer is unhappy with a decision. During Support hours, 7:00 am to midnight, an agent picks it up in the same chat.

**Can the automated assistant approve my refund?**

Yes, up to ₹500 per order, when the order is yours, has been delivered and the item is eligible under its category rules. Anything above ₹500 goes to the Customer Care Lead, who is a human and reviews the photo evidence.

**The assistant said it doesn't know. Is something wrong?**

No. Our automated assistant is required to say so plainly when it doesn't know the answer, and it must never promise something the Customer Policy doesn't allow. Ask for a human agent if you need more help.

**I'm not happy with the decision on my request. What can I do?**

Say so in the chat. When a customer is unhappy with a decision, the conversation is handed to a human agent, who looks at the request again under the Customer Policy.

**What happens if someone is abusive in a chat?**

A conversation with abusive or threatening language is handed to a human agent. We ask customers to keep chats respectful; our agents and delivery partners are expected to do the same.

**Someone called me saying they are from QuickBasket and asked for an OTP. Is that real?**

No. QuickBasket never asks for your UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or a bank OTP, in chat or anywhere else. The only code you may be asked for is the gate entry OTP of your own community, by the delivery partner at your gate. Report anything else in the in-app chat.

**Can a family member ask Support about my order?**

Not from their own account. Support discusses an order only with the account that placed it, after confirming that the order belongs to that customer.

**Does the delivery partner see my phone number?**

No. Calls and messages between you and the delivery partner go through the app, and the partner sees your delivery address and order notes only for the duration of the delivery.

**I ate something from my order and it tasted spoiled. What should I do?**

Stop using the item and keep the pack. Report it from the order page with a photo inside the window for that category: 24 hours for fresh produce and for dairy, bakery and eggs, 12 hours for frozen food and meat. If you feel unwell, please see a doctor first.

**How do I complain about a delivery partner's behaviour?**

Tell Support in the in-app chat and include the order ID. Complaints about conduct are read by a human agent, not closed by the automated assistant.

**Where can I read the full Customer Policy?**

The current version is Customer Policy v4.2, effective 1 October 2026, available under Help › Policies. If this FAQ and the Customer Policy ever differ, the Customer Policy is the one that applies.

**I already had three refunds this month and need another one. What happens?**

The support agent or the automated assistant can't approve it. A customer can have at most three refunds in any 30-day period, and a further request goes to the Customer Care Lead for a decision.

**Why did the assistant refuse to tell me about an order?**

Most likely because it could not confirm that the order belongs to the account you are signed in to. Check that you are using the account that placed the order and that the order ID (format QB-12345) is typed correctly.

**Support chat is closed. How do I report a problem at 1 am?**

Use the order page. Return, refund and missing-item requests can be raised there at any hour, and the time you raise the request is what counts for the category window. Chat reopens at 7:00 am.

**What information should I keep ready before I chat with Support?**

The order ID (format QB-12345), a photo if an item is damaged or spoiled, and the app signed in to the account that placed the order. With those three things most requests are settled in one conversation.

## Words used in this FAQ

- **Order ID:** The reference of an order, in the format QB-12345. Needed for every request.
- **Promised delivery time:** The time shown at checkout when you place the order. Lateness is measured against it.
- **Delivery time shown in the app:** The moment the order was marked delivered. Every return or report window is counted from it.
- **Category rules:** The return and report rules that apply to a category of items. They take priority over the general 7-day rule.
- **Customer-side delay:** Time lost because of a wrong address, the customer not being reachable or waiting for entry at a gated community. It is excluded when lateness is worked out.
- **Declared advisory:** A weather or traffic advisory that QuickBasket declares for a city, shown as a banner in the app.
- **QuickBasket Cash:** The wallet in the app. It holds Refund Cash, Compensation Cash and Promotional Cash.
- **Business days:** Bank working days. Weekends and public holidays are not counted.
- **Customer Care Lead:** The human who decides refund requests that a support agent or the automated assistant is not allowed to approve.
- **Superseded policy:** An older version of the Customer Policy, kept only for orders placed while it was in force.
